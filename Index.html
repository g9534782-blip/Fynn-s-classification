Flynn’s Classification of Computer Architectures

Computers are sorted by how many instruction streams and data streams they handle at once.

The two ingredients

Instruction stream (IS)
What to do: the recipe or the to-do list.
Data stream (DS)
What to do it to: the ingredients or the numbers.

One or many instructions × one or many data = four classes: SISD, SIMD, MISD, MIMD.
